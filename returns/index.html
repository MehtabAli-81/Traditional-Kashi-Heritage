THREE-DAY WINDOW

For the right reasons.

A three-day return or exchange window applies to items that are damaged, incorrect, or materially different from their description. It does not extend to general change-of-mind returns under the assignment policy.

WITHIN 24 HOURS

Show what arrived.

For delivery breakage, customers can submit photographs or an unboxing video within 24 hours. The assignment says verified delivery damage qualifies for replacement or refund.

REVIEW & RESOLUTION

Handled with care.

A real store must publish a working customer-care contact and a practical method to submit evidence before accepting orders. No case submission or refund service is connected in this demo.

NATURAL HANDMADE VARIATION

Handmade pieces can vary in brushwork and finish. The assignment calls for honest descriptions of materials and natural variations; a product materially different from its description remains covered by its stated return window.
