> **Nasarpur Kashi Heritage** is a digital B2C and D2C e-commerce platform designed to showcase and commercialize authentic, hand-painted Sindhi blue pottery (*Kashigari*). The project bridges centuries-old artisan craftsmanship from Nasarpur, Sindh with modern digital retail, providing direct market access, fair-trade pricing for artisan families, and a seamless shopping experience for global craft enthusiasts.

## 💡 Core Business & Value Proposition

- **Artisan-to-Consumer Model (B2C + D2C):** Eliminates unnecessary middlemen, giving artisans direct access to individual buyers, corporate clients, and interior designers.
- **Fair-Trade & Social Impact:** Provides sustainable and dignified income streams to 10–15 initial artisan families in Nasarpur, reinvesting profits into tools, training, and craft development.
- **Cultural Preservation:** Digitally preserves and promotes traditional blue-and-white patterns, hand-painted floral motifs, and historical Sindhi ceramics.
- **Damage-Free Guarantee:** Implements strict quality-control protocols and specialized shock-absorbent packaging for fragile goods, backed by a 24-hour breakage replacement policy.

## 🏗 System Architecture & Operations

```text
[ Artisans (Nasarpur) ] ──> [ Quality & Inspection Center ] ──> [ Digital Channels (Web/Social/WhatsApp) ]
                                                                       │
[ Delivery & Logistics ] <── [ Packaging & Fulfillment ] <──────────────┘
```

1. **Artisan Network:** Direct sourcing partnerships with local craft families in Nasarpur.
2. **Quality Control Center:** Centralized hub for checking surface integrity, dimensions, pattern consistency, and micro-cracks before dispatch.
3. **Digital Storefront:** Multi-channel storefront incorporating a Web Catalogue, Social Commerce (Instagram/Facebook Shops), and WhatsApp Business support.
4. **Payment Gateways:** Integrated digital payment options including Easypaisa, JazzCash, direct Bank Transfer, Cash on Delivery (COD), and Credit/Debit Cards.
5. **Logistics & Packaging:** Custom protective packaging engineered for fragile glazed pottery paired with reliable courier networks.

## 📊 Business & Revenue Model

### Revenue Streams
1. **Direct Sales:** E-commerce store sales priced to sustain operations and deliver healthy gross margins.
2. **Customization Services:** Additional fee (Rs. 300–500+) for custom inscriptions, family names, corporate logos, and custom color variations.
3. **Curated Bundles:** Specialized gift packages for weddings, housewarmings, Eid, and corporate events to boost Average Order Value (AOV).
4. **Artisan Marketplace Commission:** Future scalable revenue stream collecting a 15–20% service commission for third-party artisan listings.

## ⚖️ Core Policies

* **Authenticity Policy:** 100% genuine handcrafted ceramic items with full provenance disclosure.
* **Breakage Guarantee:** Unboxing video or photos within 24 hours of delivery qualifies for immediate replacement or full refund.
* **3-Day Return Window:** Applies to damaged, incorrect, or materially non-conforming items.
* **Fair-Trade Commitment:** Timely payments directly to artisans and transparent profit-sharing mechanisms.
