01OUR PURPOSE

Culture, with a clear path to the customer.

Kashi work is valued for its blue-and-white patterns, hand-painted floral motifs, and strong cultural identity. This store is designed to make the craft easier to discover and purchase while supporting dignified market access for artisan families.

The proposed model is B2C and D2C: work directly with artisans, check every item, then serve customers through a website, social shops, and WhatsApp Business. It is a way to reduce unnecessary middlemen and give each piece room to tell its story.

The assignment describes an initial network of 10–15 artisan families in Nasarpur and nearby areas. That is a planned partnership target, not a claim about an established store.

THE PROMISE BEHIND THE PIECE
01

Authentic by design

Every listed piece is intended to be handmade or artisan-produced Kashi pottery, with honest descriptions and natural variations.

02

Checked before it travels

The quality policy calls for inspection of cracks, finish, size, and customization before dispatch; fragile work is to be carefully packed.

03

Value shared fairly

The business is designed around direct partnerships, timely agreed payments, and reinvestment in tools, training, and product development.

How the model works.
